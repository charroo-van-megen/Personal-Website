Hobbies & Personal Interests

Outside of game development, I enjoy creative hobbies that help me improve storytelling, imagination, and player experience design. These interests strongly influence both my writing and gameplay philosophy.

Personal Information

I enjoy creativity, discipline, storytelling, and immersive experiences. Outside of game development, I spend time writing novels, reading fantasy and mythology, analyzing games, exercising, and experimenting with creative ideas.

These hobbies help improve my understanding of atmosphere, interaction, pacing, world-building, and emotional engagement.

Creative Writing

Writing is one of my biggest creative passions outside of game development. I enjoy designing worlds, lore, power systems, and psychologically-driven characters.

My stories often focus on mystery, progression fantasy, emotional conflict, identity, and immersive storytelling.

Gaming & Analysis

I enjoy analyzing gameplay systems, movement mechanics, progression systems, pacing, and player immersion.

Understanding what makes games satisfying helps improve my own gameplay design philosophy.

Reading

I enjoy reading fantasy, mythology, psychology, and game design related material. Reading helps improve creativity and storytelling depth.

Sports & Discipline

Sports help me maintain consistency, discipline, and focus — qualities important for long-term creative projects and teamwork.
